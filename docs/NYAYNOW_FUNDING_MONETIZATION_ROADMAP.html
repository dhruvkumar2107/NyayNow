Financing & Monetization Roadmap

A strategic guide to securing grants, attracting investment, and launching operations

Project Positioning & Value Proposition

NyayNow addresses India's acute justice deficit by providing citizens with 17 AI-backed diagnostic tools (including automated BNS 2024 compliance, local statutory mappings, and FIR document generators) and advocates with a secure practice console. Having verified 91.4% BNS intent classification accuracy across 500 advocate-reviewed cases, the platform is ready for commercialization and seed financing.

Access to Justice AI Generative Cascade LegalTech SaaS BNS/IPC Compliant
1. Equity-Free Grants & Prizes

Due to the platform's social impact potential, pursue non-dilutive financing first:

  • HiiL Justice Accelerator: Hague-based program offering €10,000 to €20,000 equity-free grants and global mentorship.
  • Agami Prize (India): Premier Indian legal innovation ecosystem offering ₹5L - ₹10L in grants and advocacy partnerships.
  • Samanway Foundation: Focuses on open-source, civic-tech tooling integrations in India.
HiiL Challenge Agami Co-Lab
2. Accelerators & Seed VCs

To scale engineering and geographic expansion, target equity partners:

  • Startup India Seed Fund: Up to ₹20 Lakhs for prototyping and ₹50 Lakhs for commercial scaling.
  • Academic Incubators: Target host institutions (e.g., IIT Madras Incubation Cell, IIMB NSRCEL) for workspace and capital.
  • Y Combinator & Antler India: Pitch the tech stack during cohorts. YC supports vertical legal SaaS; Antler provides $100K - $150K.
Startup India Y Combinator
3. Revenue & Monetization Models

Build sustainability while complying with Bar Council rules on fee sharing:

  • SaaS for Advocates: Monthly premium tiers (₹999/mo) for the Devil's Advocate, Case Timeline Analyser, and practice management tools.
  • Academic Licensing: SaaS subscriptions for Law Universities to run the AI Moot Court Evaluator.
  • Convenience Fee: Flat matchmaking fees for clients booking consults, bypassing percent commissions.
B2B SaaS University Licensing
4. Visibility & Launch Roadmap

Optimize organic acquisition channels to collect usage metrics:

  • Hacker News (Show HN): Target engineers and VCs by highlighting your RAG and prompt cascade paper.
  • Product Hunt & Peerlist: Launch to the tech community for reviews and initial directory exposure.
  • Signed LOIs (Advocate Pilot): Distribute your Letter of Intent to secure 10+ active advocate pilot signups.
