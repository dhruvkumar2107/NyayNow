Expression of Interest — NyayNow Digital Legal Platform
To The Founding Team, NyayNow
Sub. Declaration of interest and support for the NyayNow platform

I,  , Advocate, enrolled with the Bar Council of  , vide Enrolment No.  , presently practising in the field of  , with   years of standing at the Bar, write this letter to formally record my interest in the NyayNow platform — a digital legal services initiative presently under development in India.

Having reviewed the vision and proposed features of the NyayNow platform, I am of the considered opinion that it addresses a long-standing and critical gap in access to legal services within the country. A significant portion of India's population remains unaware of their legal rights and is unable to connect with qualified legal counsel in a timely or affordable manner. A structured digital platform of this nature holds the potential to fundamentally transform how legal services are discovered, accessed, and delivered.

I hereby formally record my intent that, upon the official launch of the NyayNow platform — with its proposed features including online legal consultations, appointment scheduling, and case management tools — I intend to register on the platform, pilot its services within my practice, and, where appropriate, recommend it to my peers and clients.

I am particularly supportive of the platform's objective of providing structured opportunities to junior advocates and law students. Young lawyers in India frequently face difficulty in securing meaningful exposure and clientele during the early years of their career. NyayNow's approach of connecting junior lawyers with senior advocates, facilitating mentorship, and providing a credible channel for client engagement would serve as a valuable professional resource for the next generation of legal professionals.

For the common citizen, the ability to engage a verified legal professional through a reliable digital interface — without navigating unfamiliar court complexes or relying entirely on personal references — represents a meaningful step towards the democratisation of legal assistance in India.

I clarify that this letter is a voluntary and non-binding expression of personal interest and support only. It does not constitute a contract, partnership, or financial commitment. I understand that NyayNow is not yet a registered entity, and this letter is issued solely in recognition of the initiative's merit. I extend my best wishes to the founding team.

Yours sincerely,
