CH 01

The Bento Ecosystem (Homepage Core Features)

The landing page at www.nyaynow.in highlights four flagship AI legal applications inside an interactive bento grid. This chapter details their exact operational interfaces and parameters.

Flagship Module

NyayCourt Simulator (Neural Trial Engine)

www.nyaynow.in/courtroom-battle

This module simulates a multi-round courtroom trial between three AI legal agents—Prosecution, Defense, and the Bench—to stress-test case facts.

Actual Inputs & Controls:

Field Name Type Description & Voice Option
Case Category Button Selector Options: Civil, Criminal, Property, Family, Consumer, Corporate
Case Facts & Context Textarea (Speech-to-Text) Primary facts. Built-in **Voice Entry** utilizes SpeechRecognition API.
Prosecution Position Textarea Description of demand.
Defense Position Textarea Opposition parameters.

The Operational Process:

  1. Submit facts. Click "Initialize AI Trial" (POST payload maps to /api/ai/courtroom-battle).
  2. Rounds reveal progressively using an active typewriter effect. Color bars represent speakers: Prosecution (Amber), Defense (Indigo), Judge (Rose/Red).
  3. **Verdict Reveal:** Displays a final ruling by *Hon. Justice R.K. Krishnamurthy*, win-probability split chart, decider factors, cited Indian Kanoon precedents, and the final Court Order.
Accessibility Module

NyayVoice AI Dialect Consultant

www.nyaynow.in/voice-assistant

A voice-to-voice consultation interface translating lay narratives into structured legal responses, breaking linguistic barriers.

Actual Controls & Dialect Support:

  • **Language Selection Dropdown:** English (India) [en-IN], Hindi (हिन्दी) [hi-IN], Bengali (বাংলা) [bn-IN], Tamil (தமிழ்) [ta-IN].
  • **Interactive Mic Button:** Toggles continuous recording via web speech browser interface.
  • **Voice Synthesis Feedback:** Speaks back the AI response in the selected dialect utilizing `window.speechSynthesis`.
Draft & Risk Lab

Smart Drafting & Risk Analysis Lab

www.nyaynow.in/drafting

Features two major active tabs to draft NDAs and scan agreement PDF files for hidden contractual risks.

Tab 1: Smart Draft

  • **Document Type Dropdown:** NDA, Legal Service Proposal, Employment Contract, Rent Agreement, Freelance Agreement, Legal Notice.
  • **Parties Involved & Key Terms:** Textarea with voice entry. Click **"Generate Document"** (POST request to /api/ai/draft-contract) to render markdown contract text.
  • **DocuSign e-Sign Integration:** Click **"e-Sign"** to send the contract base64 string to /api/docusign/sign, launching a secure signing envelope in a new tab.

Tab 2: Risk Analysis

  • **PDF Uploader & Text Input:** Securely upload a contract `.pdf` file (POST to /api/ai/analyze-agreement-pdf) or paste raw text.
  • **Structured AI Output:** Renders a numeric **Clarity Score** (e.g., 85/100), **Risk Assessment Level** (High/Medium/Low), bulleted lists of **Critical Loopholes** (missing clauses), and **Ambiguous Terms**.
Semantic Research

Precedent Engine (Semantic Precedent Finder)

www.nyaynow.in/research

A search console utilizing neural embeddings to locate active legal precedents from court records.

Operational Action Guide:

  1. Type a query (e.g. "Right to privacy under Article 21") and click **Search**.
  2. The backend issues a query to the model chat handler, parsing out a structured JSON block containing: Case Name, Official Citation, detailed precedent Summary, and calculated Relevance Score percentage.
CH 02

Client Cockpit & Triage Workspace

This chapter outlines the operational client cockpit interface (configured in ClientDashboard.jsx) where litigants manage active cases and request consultations.

Dashboard Tab 1

Dashboard Overview & Legal Reels

www.nyaynow.in/client/dashboard?tab=overview

The central client control center displays case timelines, invoicing ledgers, appointments, and connections.

Actual Workflows:

  1. **Active Cases Timeline:** Displays case listings dynamically fetched from /api/cases?postedBy=phone. Renders case progressions visually using a dedicated `CaseTimeline` component.
  2. **Legal Reels:** Renders a short-form video reels console (`LegalReels`) built using dynamic React imports to educate users on regional rights.
  3. **Suggested Lawyers list:** Automatically displays cards of top registered advocates. Click **"Connect"** to hit POST /api/connections.
WebSocket Room

Instant WebSocket Consultation & Video Meet Rooms

www.nyaynow.in/meet/:meetingId

The platform implements direct socket events for real-time consultation dispatch.

The WebSocket Dispatch Loop:

  1. Client clicks **"Request Instant Consultation"**. The client node emits `join_room` and notifies the server pool.
  2. The server broadcasts `incoming_lead` to all online advocates subscribed via WebSocket `join_lawyer_pool`.
  3. Upon advocate acceptance, the socket broadcasts `consult_start` with a generated unique `meetingId`.
  4. Both client and advocate are instantly redirected to www.nyaynow.in/meet/:meetingId, launching WebRTC video consultation panels.
Confession Booth

Anonymous Confession Booth

www.nyaynow.in/client/dashboard?tab=feed

An anonymous advice forum allowing citizens to post legal worries and receive AI diagnostics without storing user logs.

Operational Action Guide:

  1. Navigate to the feed tab. Submit an intake form describing a grievance.
  2. The database router /api/confessions intercepts the payload, redacts user details using query projection boundaries, and asynchronously attaches AI-generated statutory diagnostics to the post.
CH 03

Lawyer Command Center & Lead Pipeline

This chapter details the advocate operations dashboard (LawyerDashboard.jsx) where legal professionals manage clients, verify credentials, and invoice fees.

Bar Onboarding

DigiLocker Bar Verification

www.nyaynow.in/digilocker-verify

The platform enforces strict practitioner verification. Simple payments do not verify an advocate.

Operational Action Guide:

  1. Go to `www.nyaynow.in/digilocker-verify`. Select your State Bar Council and input your Bar Council registration number.
  2. The system triggers a secure government gateway handshake contract. Upon successful callback verification of your credentials, the database sets user status to `verified: true` and unlocks bidding rights on the Lead Pool.
Practice Tabs

Kanban Workload & Client CRM Insights

www.nyaynow.in/lawyer/dashboard?tab=board

Advocates organize active matters using advanced workflow tools.

Key Workspace Tabs:

  1. **Kanban Board (board):** A task widget (`KanbanBoard`) where advocates drag-and-drop client milestones (e.g. "Written Statement", "Evidence Filing").
  2. **Client CRM (crm):** Displays a client relationship analytics console (`ClientCRM`) dynamically synced with backend insights from /api/crm/insights.
  3. **Notice Generator (notices):** Includes an integrated notices drafter (`LegalNoticeGenerator`) to compile pre-litigation notices based on form parameters.
Escrow Ledger

Milestone Escrow Invoicing & Payment Gates

www.nyaynow.in/payment

Advocates secure and track their consulting and retainer fees utilizing Razorpay payment gateways and secure escrow ledger slots.

Operational Action Guide:

  1. In the billing panel, click "Create Milestone Invoice".
  2. Fill in the fields: Client ID (dropdown), Amount, and Milestone Description. Click send.
  3. The system writes the record to the `Invoice` collection (POST payload to /api/invoices). When paid, the funds are held in secure escrow until the case milestone is verified and released.
