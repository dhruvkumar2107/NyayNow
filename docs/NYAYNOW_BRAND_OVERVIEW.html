🤝 About Us

Who We Are — In Human Words

"NyayNow is building the world's most advanced legal operating system for India. We bridge the gap between complex legal systems and 1.4 billion people who deserve access to justice."

— About Page, nyaynow.in

NyayNow is India's first AI-powered legal platform — and we mean actually built for India 🇮🇳. Not a Western chatbot with an Indian flag slapped on top. We're trained on BNS, BNSS, BSA, IPC, CrPC, CPC, and every major Indian state law — from the ground up.

Here's the real problem: India has 1 lawyer for every 1,500 citizens and 4.5 crore+ cases clogging up the courts. Most people can't afford legal help, don't know their rights, and have zero idea where to start when trouble hits.

We put an entire legal ecosystem — AI-powered guidance, verified lawyer marketplace, courtroom simulators, emergency SOS, document drafting, and more — into one platform that anyone can use. In their own language. At a price they can actually afford.
