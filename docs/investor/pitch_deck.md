# NyayNow — 10-slide investor narrative (draft)

- Build toward legal answers and workflows linked to the source passage and applicable date.
- Current product is an early workflow foundation; the source-verification layer is in development.
- Goal: make legal information more inspectable, multilingual, and useful to advocates and citizens.
