# Demo, outreach, and grant narrative

## 90-second demo script

**0–15 sec:** “A legal answer depends on the source, jurisdiction, and date. NyayNow is building a workflow that makes those dependencies inspectable.”

**15–35 sec:** Show the homepage local demo. Say clearly: “This is a UI prototype; this question is not sent to a model. Production source receipts are not enabled.”

**35–55 sec:** Show a search result only if a live configured source record is available. Open the original source and confirm the passage/date. If unavailable, show the methodology page and explain what the proof record will contain.

**55–75 sec:** Show an editable document draft and its review warning; do not claim filing, enforceability, or advocate approval.

**75–90 sec:** “Our next proof point is a permissioned corpus and counsel-reviewed citation benchmark with design partners. We will report errors, abstentions, and costs before claiming quality.”

## Follow-up

After 5–7 business days, send one short follow-up with a specific question or new piece of evidence. Stop after one unanswered follow-up. Do not attach personal case records.

## Grant narrative (draft)

NyayNow proposes to develop a lawful, versioned Indian legal source layer that links generated legal information to exact source passages and the dates/jurisdictions for which they apply. Funding would support source-rights review, corpus and temporal-rule engineering, counsel-reviewed benchmark design, privacy/security controls, and consented design-partner evaluation. Current software provides an application/API foundation and proof-record scaffolding; production corpus coverage, entailment verification, and measured performance are not yet established. Milestones and budget should be supplied only after quotations, team plan, and applicant eligibility are confirmed.
