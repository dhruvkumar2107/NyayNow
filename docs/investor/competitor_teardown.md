## Ten possible differentiators to prove

1. Passage-level evidence references.
2. Temporal statute validity and amendment traceability.
3. Jurisdiction and court filters.
4. Citation existence and passage-match verification.
5. Explicit abstention on weak evidence.
6. Multilingual/code-switching review protocol.
7. Permissioned corpus provenance and deletion controls.
8. Expert-reviewed error taxonomy and reproducible benchmark.
9. Human review workflow and correction capture.
10. Auditable costs and latency per successful task.

These are roadmap hypotheses, not current comparative advantages.
